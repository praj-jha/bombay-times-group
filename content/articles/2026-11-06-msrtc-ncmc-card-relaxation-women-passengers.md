The **Maharashtra State Road Transport Corporation (MSRTC)** has relaxed its rule making the **National Common Mobility Card (NCMC)** mandatory for women travelling at concessional fares. From **November 7 until December 31**, women passengers can continue to avail the concession **without the card**.

## Why the rule was relaxed

The NCMC became compulsory from **October 1** for women and senior citizens using concessional fares on ST buses. But passengers — particularly in rural areas, where awareness and access to registration are limited — reported trouble registering, activating and using the cards.

MSRTC has registered and distributed around **1.10 crore NCMC cards** so far, but a large number of eligible women have not completed the process.

## Revenue drop

The corporation's **average daily earnings fell from ₹33 crore in September to ₹28 crore in October**, a drop of about ₹5 crore a day, according to the *Free Press Journal*. MSRTC has not officially linked the decline to the card requirement, but passenger difficulties with the new rule have come under scrutiny.

## Special camps in villages

To clear the backlog, MSRTC will hold **registration and card-distribution camps at the gram panchayat level** with the help of local representatives, passenger associations and panchayat officials.

**What it means for passengers:** Women who do not yet have an NCMC card can keep travelling at concessional fares until December 31, but should register at a depot or camp before the relaxation ends.
