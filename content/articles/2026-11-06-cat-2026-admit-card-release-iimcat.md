The **Indian Institute of Management (IIM) Indore** is scheduled to release the **Common Admission Test (CAT) 2026 admit cards today, November 6**. Registered candidates will be able to download their hall tickets from the official website, **iimcat.ac.in**.

**CAT 2026 will be held on Sunday, November 29, in three sessions.** The exam is the gateway to MBA and other management programmes at the IIMs and hundreds of other business schools.

## How to download the CAT 2026 admit card

1. Go to **iimcat.ac.in**.
2. Click **"Registered Candidate Login"**.
3. Enter your **user ID and password**.
4. Click the **admit card** link on your dashboard.
5. **Download and print** the admit card. Keep more than one copy.

## Check these details carefully

Your admit card will show your **name, photograph, signature, registration number, exam date, session (slot), reporting time and test centre address**. If anything is wrong — especially your photograph, name or test centre — contact the CAT helpdesk immediately through the official website.

## Exam-day reminders

- Carry a **printed copy of the admit card** and an **original, valid photo ID** (as specified in the CAT instructions).
- Reach the centre **well before the reporting time** printed on your admit card. Visit the centre a day or two in advance if you can.
- Read the **instructions on the admit card** for items that are allowed and not allowed inside the exam hall.

For official updates, rely only on **iimcat.ac.in**. Bombay Times Group will update this story once the download link is live.
