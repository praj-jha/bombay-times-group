Mumbaikars heading to **Zaveri Bazaar** and neighbourhood jewellers on **Dhanteras today, Friday, November 6**, will find gold close to record territory in rupee terms, although well below the peak it hit earlier this year in international markets.

## Gold and silver rates going into Dhanteras

On Thursday evening, **24-carat gold** was quoted at about **₹1,46,600 per 10 grams** in Mumbai's bullion market and **silver (999)** at about **₹1,92,600 per kg**, according to All India Bullion. Rates published by the **India Bullion and Jewellers Association (IBJA)** put 24-carat gold at **₹1,44,129 per 10 grams** — up about ₹3,000 from the previous day — and silver at **₹1,87,300 per kg**.

That makes gold roughly **9% dearer than on Dhanteras 2025**, when 24-carat gold was around ₹1,33,866 per 10 grams. Internationally, spot gold was trading at around **$4,060–4,115 an ounce** this week, well off its **record of $5,594.82 an ounce on January 29, 2026**.

*Rates change through the day and differ between sources and cities. The prices above exclude GST and making charges; check your jeweller's live rate before buying.*

## Buying gold this Dhanteras: a quick checklist

1. **Insist on BIS hallmarking.** Every hallmarked piece carries a six-digit **HUID** code. You can verify it on the government's **BIS Care** app.
2. **Know the purity.** 22-carat (916) is standard for jewellery; 24-carat is used for coins and bars.
3. **Ask for the making charges separately.** They can range widely by design and jeweller, and are often negotiable during the festive season.
4. **Factor in 3% GST** on the value of gold, plus GST on making charges.
5. **Get an itemised bill** showing weight, purity, rate per gram, making charges and HUID.
6. **Compare with digital options.** Gold ETFs and gold mutual funds let you buy small amounts without making charges or storage worries.

Dhanteras marks the start of the five-day Diwali festival and is considered an auspicious day to buy gold, silver and utensils.
