Residents of **Gorai and Manori** have questioned the BMC's plans to clear land for its proposed **desalination plant**, alleging serious discrepancies in the civic body's tree count and land records at a public hearing held before the **BMC Tree Authority** at Byculla on **November 3**.

The first phase of the plant is planned to produce **200 million litres of water a day (MLD)** at a cost of about **₹3,200 crore**. The BMC is also planning a second desalination plant at **Versova**.

## 971 trees or more than 4,000?

According to the BMC, **971 trees** stand on roughly 12 acres at the Manori site. Of these, it proposes to **fell 28, transplant 939 and retain four**, with **9,610 saplings** to be planted as compensation in Palghar — though a plot for that has not yet been identified.

Villagers dispute those numbers. They say **more than 4,000 trees** stand on the land, and that some were never numbered or included in the inventory.

## "Presented as a barren 20-acre plot"

Switsy Henriques, president of the **Gorai Villagers Welfare Association**, said documents show the project was described differently to different authorities. "The project has been presented to authorities on the basis of a barren 20-acre plot and only 25 trees," she said, referring to the minutes of a **Maharashtra Coastal Zone Management Authority** meeting in March and papers submitted for Coastal Regulation Zone clearance. "The authorities owe the public a complete and transparent explanation."

Residents also pointed out that permissions refer to **20 acres**, while the tree-felling proposal covers **29 acres**, and that the BMC holds only advance possession of the land. They demanded an **independent tree census** and a joint physical inspection.

## Questions beyond trees

Speakers said the hearing dealt only with tree felling, not the plant's wider environmental impact or economic viability. Among the concerns raised:

- **Water losses:** Gaurang Vora of Save Mumbai Mangroves said about **34% of BMC's water supply is lost** to leakage and theft, and that plugging these losses could partly close the city's deficit.
- **Alternatives:** Desmond Paul of the Gorai Shetkari Vikas Sanstha suggested fixing leaks and pursuing the **Gargai and Kalu dam** projects instead.
- **Marine life and fishing:** Residents questioned claims that seawater intake and brine discharge would not harm marine life, and sought an independent study by the **Central Marine Fisheries Research Institute**. Paul said the project threatens fishermen's livelihoods.

**Jitendra Pardeshi**, Superintendent of the Garden and Tree Authority, said a decision would be taken only after the concerns raised were verified. Sambhaji Masulkar of the BMC's Water Supply Projects department explained the city's need for desalination at the hearing.
