Defending champions **India** came from behind to beat **Japan 4-1** in the final of the **Asian Champions Trophy** at the **Olympian Balbir Singh Senior International Hockey Stadium in Mohali** on Thursday night, winning the title for a **record sixth time** and completing a **hat-trick of consecutive titles**.

## Late blitz after early shock

Japan stunned the home crowd in the **3rd minute** when **Kazumasa Matsumoto** scored with a brilliant backhand. India dominated possession but could not find a way through until the third quarter.

The equaliser finally came in the **42nd minute**, when **Shilanand Lakra** finished a penalty-corner variation set up by captain **Harmanpreet Singh**. India then ran riot in the final quarter:

| Minute | Scorer | Score |
|---|---|---|
| 3' | Kazumasa Matsumoto (JPN) | 0-1 |
| 42' | Shilanand Lakra (IND) | 1-1 |
| 47' | Dilpreet Singh (IND) | 2-1 |
| 52' | Jugraj Singh (IND), drag-flick | 3-1 |
| 53' | Vivek Sagar Prasad (IND) | 4-1 |

## Perfect campaign

India finished the tournament **unbeaten with seven wins from seven matches**, having also beaten Japan 4-1 in the league stage at the Olympian Surjit Hockey Stadium in Jalandhar.

India's six Asian Champions Trophy titles came in **2011, 2016, 2018 (shared), 2023, 2024 and 2026**. For Japan, it was a third final defeat — after 2013 and 2021 — and they remain without a maiden title.

## Malaysia take bronze

Earlier, **Malaysia** came from 1-2 down to beat **South Korea 5-2** in the third-place match. **Abu Kamal Azrai** struck twice, in the 45th and 50th minutes, and was named Man of the Match, while captain **Marhan Jalil** sealed the win with a 58th-minute penalty corner.
