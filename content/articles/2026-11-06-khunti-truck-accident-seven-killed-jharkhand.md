**Seven people, including three children, were killed** and one woman was injured when a speeding truck ran over a group of people standing by the roadside in Jharkhand's **Khunti district** on **Thursday night**, police said.

The accident took place at around **9:45 pm** near the **Jone–Ludru turn on the Karra–Torpa main road**, within the limits of Karra police station.

## Crowd had gathered after a scooter crash

Local residents had gathered at the spot after an earlier accident involving a scooter rider. Police said an **overspeeding truck** then drove into the crowd. Six people died on the spot and one more died during treatment.

Khunti Superintendent of Police **Rishabh Garg** said the dead included **three women, three children and one man**, according to reports. All of the victims lived in a colony near the Jone–Ludru turn. The injured woman is being treated at **Khunti Sadar Hospital**.

## Driver absconding

The **truck driver and his helper fled** after the accident. Some residents alleged that the driver was drunk; police have not confirmed this. Officers said efforts were under way to trace the driver and that further action would follow.

The accident is a grim reminder of the danger of **secondary crashes**, in which bystanders who stop to help after one accident are hit by another vehicle. Road safety experts advise those helping at a crash site to stay off the carriageway, switch on hazard lights and warn oncoming traffic wherever possible.
