**Ajay Devgn's *Drishyam 3*** has crossed **₹590 crore at the worldwide box office** after more than a month in cinemas, making it one of the biggest Hindi hits of the year.

## The numbers

According to trade tracker **Koimoi**, the film's collections after **34 days** stand at:

| | Collection |
|---|---|
| India (net) | ₹379.6 crore |
| India (gross) | ₹447.97 crore |
| Overseas (gross) | ₹144.89 crore |
| **Worldwide (gross)** | **₹592.86 crore** |

Koimoi puts the film's budget at about **₹110 crore** and has declared it a "super-duper hit".

*Box office figures in India are not officially audited and vary between trackers. Bollymoviereviewz, for instance, puts the worldwide total lower, at about ₹561 crore.*

## Diwali celebrations

The film's success was toasted at one of several Diwali-week parties in Mumbai on **Wednesday, November 4**, as the industry marked a strong festive season at the box office.

## The franchise

*Drishyam 3* is billed as the concluding chapter of the Hindi franchise, with Devgn returning as **Vijay Salgaonkar**, the cable operator who goes to extraordinary lengths to protect his family. The Hindi films are adaptations of the Malayalam originals starring **Mohanlal**; the Malayalam *Drishyam 3* released earlier, on **May 21, 2026**.
