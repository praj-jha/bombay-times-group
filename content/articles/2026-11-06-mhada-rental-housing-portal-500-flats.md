The Maharashtra Housing and Area Development Authority (**MHADA**) plans to launch its long-awaited **rental housing portal by the end of November**, starting with **more than 500 unsold flats** from its housing lotteries in Mumbai, the *Free Press Journal* reported.

The portal will let prospective tenants see property details — location, rent and facilities — online, and is meant to reduce the city's dependence on brokers and middlemen, lowering brokerage costs and making the search for a home more transparent.

## A single window for tenants and landlords

MHADA intends the platform to work as one digital interface for tenants and landlords and to link it with other services over time. An **e-court facility to settle rental disputes** is being developed for the portal.

A recent government notification designates **Divisional Commissioners of Police** as the competent authorities for rental housing disputes in Mumbai, and MHADA is working towards handling such disputes through the portal.

## Private developers may join

MHADA is also exploring whether **private developers' vacant or unsold homes** can be listed for rent on the platform, and has held discussions with the developers' bodies **CREDAI-MCHI** and **NAREDCO**.

## Part of a wider rental push for MMR

The portal is one piece of the state government's effort to build a formal rental market in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR). Under the proposed rental housing policy:

- **30–40% of homes** in MMR housing projects could be earmarked for rent
- Rent of up to **₹15,000 a month** is proposed for homes of up to **400 sq ft**
- Incentives would encourage private developers to participate
- Affordable rental homes are envisaged within **about 15 minutes of railway and Metro stations**, aimed at working professionals, students and migrant workers

MHADA has not yet announced eligibility criteria, the application process or the rents for the first batch of flats. Bombay Times Group will update this story when the portal opens.
