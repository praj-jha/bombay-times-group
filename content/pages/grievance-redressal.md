Bombay Times Group follows the **Code of Ethics** prescribed for publishers of news and current affairs content under the **Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021**.

## How to file a grievance

Send an email to the Grievance Officer with:

1. Your name and contact details
2. The link (URL) to the content concerned
3. The nature of your grievance and the part of the Code of Ethics you believe has been breached
4. Any supporting documents

## Timelines

We will **acknowledge your grievance within 24 hours** and aim to **dispose of it within 15 days** of receipt, communicating our decision to you. If you are not satisfied with the decision, you may appeal to the self-regulating body of which we are a member, as provided under the Rules.
