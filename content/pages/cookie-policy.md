Cookies are small text files stored on your device by websites you visit.

## Cookies we use

- **Essential/preferences:** we store your light or dark mode choice in your browser's local storage. This never leaves your device.
- **Analytics (if enabled):** tools such as Google Analytics help us understand how readers use the site.
- **Advertising (if enabled):** advertising partners such as Google may use cookies to show and measure ads, which may be personalised based on your interests.

## Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can opt out of personalised Google ads at [adssettings.google.com](https://adssettings.google.com). Blocking some cookies may affect how the site works.
