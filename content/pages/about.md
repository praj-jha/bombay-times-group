**Bombay Times Group** is an independent digital news publication based in **Mumbai**. We report on the city we live in — its civic life, transport, housing, environment, courts and culture — and on the national and international stories that matter to Mumbaikars.

## What we cover

- **Mumbai** — BMC and civic affairs, local trains and Metro, roads, housing, crime, courts, weather and air quality across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and Thane
- **India** — national politics, the Supreme Court, governance and policy
- **Business** — markets, company results, banking and insurance, gold prices and personal finance
- **Sports, Entertainment, Education, Tech and Lifestyle**
- **World** — international affairs through an Indian lens

## How we work

We verify before we publish. Every story names its sources, and where we build on reporting by other news organisations or official documents, we link to them. We correct mistakes promptly and transparently. Read our [Editorial Standards](/editorial-policy/) and [Corrections Policy](/corrections-policy/) for details.

Photographs on our site are either our own, supplied by the people or organisations pictured, or used under free licences (such as Creative Commons) with full credit to the photographer.
