We work hard to get things right, but when we make a mistake we correct it quickly and openly.

## How to report an error

Email **{{email}}** with the link to the story and a description of the error. Please include any supporting evidence. We aim to respond within **48 hours**.

## How we correct

- **Factual errors** are corrected in the story and a note is added at the end explaining what was changed and when, for example: *"Correction (November 6, 2026): An earlier version of this story misstated the number of notices issued. It was 428, not 482."*
- **Clarifications** are added when a story was accurate but could be misunderstood or lacked important context.
- **Updates** to developing stories carry an "Updated" timestamp; they are not corrections.
- We do not silently delete or rewrite published stories. Stories are removed only in exceptional cases, such as legal requirements or serious safety concerns, and we will say so.

## Grievances

Complaints under the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, can be made through our [Grievance Redressal](/grievance-redressal/) process.
