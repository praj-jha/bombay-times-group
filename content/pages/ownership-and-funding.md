## Ownership

Bombay Times Group is an independent, privately owned digital news publication based in Mumbai, Maharashtra, India.

## Funding

We are funded through **advertising** and, in future, reader **subscriptions and memberships**. Any sponsored or partner content is clearly labelled and is kept separate from our editorial work.

## Independence

No advertiser, sponsor or investor has any say over what we cover or how we cover it. Bombay Times Group is **not affiliated with The Times of India, Bennett, Coleman & Co. Ltd. or any other media organisation**.
